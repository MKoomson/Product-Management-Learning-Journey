# Product Management Learning Journey

Welcome to my Product Management Learning Journey!

This repository documents my transition into Product Management through structured learning, practical exercises, product case studies, and real-world problem-solving.

My goal is not only to learn Product Management concepts but also to demonstrate my ability to think like a Product Manager by applying industry frameworks, analyzing existing products, and building a portfolio of product work.

---

## Objectives

Throughout this journey, I aim to:

* Develop a strong foundation in Product Management principles.
* Learn industry-standard frameworks used by Product Managers.
* Practice solving real product problems through case studies.
* Build portfolio-ready product documents and projects.
* Prepare for Product Management interviews.
* Continuously document my learning and growth.

---

Designing and documenting products from problem discovery through launch strategy using Product Management best practices.

* Product Design Questions

* Metrics Questions
* Prioritization Scenarios
* Mock Product Manager Interviews

---

## Learning Philosophy

Throughout this journey, I follow one guiding principle:

> **Great Product Managers don't start by asking, "What should we build?" They start by asking, "What problem are we solving?"**

Each lesson emphasizes understanding customer problems before proposing solutions.
